Andiamo

Privacy policy

Updated November 25, 2026

The short version

Andiamo lists free events. You can browse without an account. We collect only what's needed to sign you in, show your events and keep the app safe. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.

What we collect

Your email address, to sign you in. It is never shown to other people. If you sign in with Apple, you can choose to share a private relay address instead.

If you sign in with Apple or Google, the name and email they share with us.

The name you choose to show as a host or attendee, if you enter one.

Events you create, including their address, meeting link and any links you add. Event details are public, except the meeting link, which only people who sign up can see.

Which events you sign up for. The host can see your display name.

Reports you send and hosts you block.

Location

If you tap Use my location, your phone finds your approximate area so the app can show nearby events. It's rounded to about a kilometer, kept on your phone, and never sent to Andiamo. Your phone's map service may look up the name of your city. When you host an in-person event, the address you enter is turned into map coordinates by your phone's map service and stored with the event.

Who handles your data

Andiamo's database, sign-in and sign-in emails run on Supabase. Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google are handled by Apple and Google. Address lookups use your phone's built-in map service from Apple or Google. Links you open, such as Discord, Zoom or social media, are covered by those companies' own policies.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile and tap Delete account. That permanently deletes your login, profile, the events you host, your signups, reports and blocks. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

Andiamo isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions or deletion requests: use the support link on the app's store listing.
